# Senior AI Engineer, Clinical Workflows

**Northwind Health** · Remote (Americas) · Full-time

Northwind builds software that helps clinics cut the paperwork around patient care. We're adding LLM features across the product, starting with intake document processing and an assistant for care coordinators, and we're hiring a senior engineer to own them end to end.

## What you'll do

- Design and ship LLM features in production: retrieval-augmented generation over clinical documents, structured extraction, and agent workflows with human review.
- Build the evaluation and monitoring that tells us when quality drops.
- Work across our stack: Python services, a TypeScript/React front end, and PostgreSQL.
- Partner with product and clinical staff to turn messy workflows into reliable features.
- Mentor other engineers and raise the bar on code review.

## What we're looking for

- 6+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong Python, including async web APIs (FastAPI or similar)
- Hands-on experience building RAG systems or LLM agents that real users depend on
- Experience with PostgreSQL, ideally including pgvector or another vector store
- Comfort with TypeScript and React
- CI/CD and cloud infrastructure experience (AWS or GCP)
- Experience with healthcare data or another regulated domain

## Nice to have

- Experience with HIPAA compliance
- LangGraph or similar agent frameworks
- Go
- Kubernetes in production

We offer competitive pay, equity, and a flexible schedule. Northwind is an equal opportunity employer.
